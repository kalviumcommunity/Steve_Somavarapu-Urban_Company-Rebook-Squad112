# **Team Charter: Urban Company One-Click Re-booking**

# **Project Problem Statement**

Urban Company seeks to streamline the user experience by implementing a **One-Click Re-booking** feature for past services. The objective is to enable customers to instantly create a new booking using the same service professional from their history.

**Key Functional Requirements:**

* **Instant Context:** Customer details must load in parallel with booking history to ensure zero-latency perception.  
* **Professional Availability:** Service professionals require a dynamic calendar view with blocked slots to prevent double-booking.  
* **Automation:** The system should automatically map previous service parameters to the new booking request.

# **Working Agreements**

To maintain high velocity and code quality, the team adheres to the following protocols:

## **PR Review Turnaround**

* **Standard:** All Pull Requests must be reviewed within **12 hours** of submission.  
* **Software Block Phase:** During critical development blocks, the target turnaround is reduced to **4 hours**.

## **Handling Blockers**

* **Immediate Action:** Raise any critical blockers in the daily standup.  
* **Communication:** Ping teammates on WhatsApp or Slack as soon as a hurdle is identified.  
* **Solo Protocol:** Attempt solo debugging for at least **30 minutes** before escalating the issue for team assistance.

## **Standup Format**

Daily check-ins will follow a strict three-point agenda for each member:

1. **Yesterday:** What was accomplished?  
2. **Today:** What is the plan for the next 24 hours?  
3. **Blockers:** What is currently impeding progress?
